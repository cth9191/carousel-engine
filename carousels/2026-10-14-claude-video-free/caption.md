Claude Code can make real videos now, and it costs nothing extra.

It's not an AI video generator. Claude writes code that draws every frame (60 a second), a hidden copy of Chrome called Playwright draws them, and FFmpeg stitches them into an MP4. Then it pulls a contact sheet of its own frames, fixes what's wrong and re-renders.

What free means: no video software, no AI video credits. Playwright and FFmpeg are free and open source. It runs on the Claude plan you already pay for, so it does count toward your usage limits. Voice-over is optional.

Two tips from my tests:
1. Ask for a storyboard first. A full render can take 20+ minutes, so approve the key frames before it builds.
2. Effort isn't the problem, your prompt is. Even low effort made a solid 15-second reel in about 20 minutes.
